# Cleaner guide

## Getting started

1. Open the app on your phone or computer and sign in at `/login` with your
   organization code, email, and the temporary password from your administrator.
2. Enter the 6-digit verification code from your email if prompted, then set a
   new password. Your NIC is identity data only — it is never your password.
3. You land on **Tasks**, which shows today's assignments. Use the bottom tab
   bar on mobile to switch between Tasks and History.
4. Use the moon/sun button to switch between light and dark mode. The top bar
   shows when data was last updated; **Refresh** reloads it on demand.

## Tasks (`/cleaner/tasks`)

- Each card shows the task name, start/finish window, room details, and any
  custom fields your supervisor added (for example, room notes).
- Use **Start** when you begin, then **Finish** when you are done. Fill in any
  required custom field values before finishing.
- Start and finish times are shown in 12-hour format in your local timezone.
- Filter by status to see all, pending, active, or completed tasks. Long lists
  use a **Show more** button.
- For photo evidence, use the upload control. The upload shows a progress bar
  and retries automatically after a transient failure. Keep the page open until
  it finishes. Only image types declared by the application are accepted.

## History (`/cleaner/history`)

- Choose a date to see your tasks for that day, including start/finish times and
  links to uploaded photos.
- Long lists use a **Show more** button.

## Helpful to know

- If you return to the tab after a while, the app checks whether data is stale
  and refreshes it. Changes made by supervisors also appear automatically.
- Error messages are written in plain language. If a message says your session
  expired, sign in again. If it says you do not have access, ask your
  administrator to check your role.
- Do not share your password. Administrators never need it, and it is never
  shown again after it is set.
